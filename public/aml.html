Compliance & AML Directives

EFFECTIVE REVISION: April 10, 2026 • PROTOCOL ID: CPT-AML-2026

CryptoPlexTrade is strictly committed to preventing the use of its digital asset exchange infrastructure for money laundering, terrorist financing, sanction evasion, and illicit financial activities. This policy outlines our operational mechanisms to maintain complete alignment with international financial guidelines and Financial Action Task Force (FATF) standards.

Zero Tolerance Policy: CryptoPlexTrade maintains zero tolerance for criminal exploitation of digital asset markets. We actively block sanctioned addresses, freeze illicit flows, and cooperate with law enforcement agencies worldwide.

1. Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

We execute multi-tiered Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Know Your Customer (KYC) verifications before granting transactional capabilities:

  • Identity Verification: Collection and automated algorithmic verification of government-issued passports, national identity cards, or driver licenses.
  • Proof of Address: Verification of official utility bills, bank statements, or municipal notices issued within the last 90 days.
  • Sanctions & PEP Screening: Automated screening against global sanctions rosters, including OFAC, UN, EU, and UK sanctions registers, as well as Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) databases.
  • Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD): High-volume trading participants and institutional entities are subject to mandatory source-of-wealth and source-of-funds verification.

2. Transaction Monitoring

Orders and payment activity may be reviewed as part of account, payment, and transaction processing:

  • Account and transaction details may be checked when needed to review an order.
  • Customers may be asked for additional information or documentation.
  • Orders may be delayed or declined while a review is pending.

3. Regulatory Reporting

CryptoPlexTrade complies with statutory obligations to submit Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) and Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs) to appropriate Financial Intelligence Units (FIUs) and designated regulatory authorities. Under statutory "tipping-off" prohibitions, our staff is legally barred from disclosing that a suspicious activity report has been lodged.

4. Prohibited Jurisdictions & Acts

Users are strictly prohibited from utilizing CryptoPlexTrade services for any illicit purpose, including:

  • Transacting on behalf of individuals or entities subject to international sanctions regimes.
  • Employing obfuscation tools, mixers, privacy chains, or tumbler contracts to conceal origin of funds.
  • Operating accounts using fictitious, synthetic, or stolen identity credentials.
  • Accessing services from embargoed countries or sanctioned geographic territories.

5. Compliance Governance

CryptoPlexTrade designates a qualified Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) responsible for overseeing adherence to this AML program, managing internal audits, and training operational staff. For compliance audits or regulatory inquiries, submit an official inquiry via our secure client Help Desk.
