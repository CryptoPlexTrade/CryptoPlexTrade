Institutional Privacy Framework

EFFECTIVE REVISION: April 10, 2026 • PROTOCOL ID: CPT-PRIV-2026

CryptoPlexTrade ("we," "our," or "us") is firmly committed to protecting your privacy and confidential transactional records. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, safeguard, and manage your information whenever you interact with our trading platform, APIs, or customer support channels.

Zero Commercial Sale Guarantee: We never sell, monetize, or lease your personal identity data, financial holdings, or transaction records to third-party data brokers or marketing affiliates.

1. Information We Collect

In order to provide compliant digital currency brokerage and exchange services, we collect information you provide directly or generate through usage:

  • Identity & Profile Data: Full legal name, date of birth, residential address, telephone number, and government-issued identification documents required for KYC validation.
  • Financial & Payment Data: Bank account transfer details, fiat clearing identifiers, and public blockchain deposit/withdrawal addresses.
  • Technical & Session Data: IP addresses, browser fingerprint, device operating system, login timestamps, and session security cookies.

2. How We Use Information

We process your data strictly to operate our platform securely and in accordance with international financial standards:

  • To provide, process, settle, and clear your cryptocurrency buy and sell orders.
  • To generate transaction confirmations, verifiable payout receipts, and legal ledger records.
  • To perform continuous automated fraud prevention, anti-money laundering (AML) checks, and security audits.
  • To communicate mission-critical account alerts, security notices, and platform updates.
  • To optimize trading engine execution velocity and improve platform usability.

3. Sharing & Disclosures

We strictly limit third-party data transmission to essential operational partners bound by rigorous non-disclosure agreements:

  • Regulated Payment Rails: Banking partners and authorized fiat payment clearing processors facilitating deposits and payouts.
  • Identity Verification Providers: Automated AML/KYC screening networks that verify legal documentation against official databases.
  • Statutory Authorities: Regulatory bodies and law enforcement agencies only when legally mandated by a valid judicial subpoena or statutory order.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement multi-tiered institutional security controls to protect your information against unauthorized access, destruction, or disclosure. All sensitive data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 cryptographic suites and secured at rest with AES-256 military-grade encryption within isolated virtual private clusters.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and CCPA frameworks), you maintain specific statutory rights regarding your personal records:

  • The right to inspect and access copies of the personal data we maintain.
  • The right to rectify inaccurate or outdated personal profile information via your Profile Dashboard.
  • The right to request data erasure, subject to mandatory regulatory retention periods governing financial transactions and AML records.

6. Contact Data Protection

For questions or requests regarding your personal records or data governance practices, please reach our Data Protection Officer at Supportteam@cryptoplextrades.com or open a ticket through our Support Desk.
