Common Operational Topics

How do blockchain block confirmations affect order release?

For sell orders, payout execution starts automatically upon achieving required consensus depth (1 confirmation for BTC, 12 for ETH/ERC20, 20 for TRC20).

What should I do if my deposit hasn't appeared?

Double check the destination network (e.g., TRC20 vs. ERC20). If the transaction hash is confirmed on-chain, open a ticket via My Tickets with your hash for immediate manual reconciliation.

How can I increase my daily trading limits?

Complete Tier 1 Identity Verification in your Profile. Higher OTC limits can be granted upon corporate documentation review.
