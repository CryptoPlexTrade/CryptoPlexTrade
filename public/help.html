Knowledge Base & Guides

8 Guides Available

Buying crypto is simple and takes just a few steps:

  1. Open Trade, choose Buy, and select an available asset and network.
  2. Enter the USD-equivalent volume or GHS amount and review the conversion and network fee.
  3. Enter and verify your destination wallet address.
  4. Continue to the order instructions and use the payment method shown for your order.
  5. Submit the payment reference when requested, then follow the recorded order status.

To sell cryptocurrency for Ghana Cedis (GH₵):

  1. Choose Sell, select an available asset and network, then enter the crypto quantity.
  2. Review the estimated GHS payout calculated from the current market price and configured sell rate.
  3. Continue to the order summary and send the quoted amount to the deposit address shown.
  4. Submit the transaction hash (TXID), then provide your payout details when prompted.
  5. Follow the latest recorded order status in transaction history.

Timing depends on payment processing, network confirmation, and order review. Follow the latest status in transaction history; contact support if you need help with a pending order.

The trade form and order review show the configured conversion and applicable network fee. Final order values are calculated using the active server rates when the order is submitted.

To comply with local financial regulations, users can complete Tier 1 identity verification by uploading a clear photo of their Ghana Card, National Passport, or Driver's License on the Profile page. Verification is typically reviewed within 2 to 6 hours.

We strongly recommend enabling 2FA. Go to your Account Profile, click "Security", and scan the QR code using Google Authenticator or Authy. Once verified, each sign-in will require a one-time 6-digit passcode.

Because blockchain transactions are irreversible once broadcast, please double-check your wallet address before submitting. If your order has not yet been processed by the desk, immediately open Live Chat or call us at 0246556706 with your Order ID so our operators can hold the transfer.

We support MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash (formerly Vodafone Cash), and AT Money. For bank transfers, we support all major licensed Ghanaian banks including GCB Bank, Ecobank, Stanbic Bank, Absa, and Zenith Bank.
