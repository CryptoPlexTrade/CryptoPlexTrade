Account & KYC Verification
How do I open an institutional or retail account?
Create an account with the requested registration details, then sign in to access the trade form. Any additional verification requirements will be shown during your account or order flow.
What is required for KYC identity verification?
If identity verification is required, the account flow will show which information or documents to provide. Review status and contact support if you have questions about a pending review.
How do I reset my account credentials if locked?
Navigate to the Sign In page and select "Forgot Password". A cryptographically signed single-use reset link will be sent to your registered email address.
Trading & Settlement Engine
How long does order settlement take?
Timing depends on payment processing, network confirmation, and order review. Follow the latest status in transaction history; contact support if you need help with a pending order.
Which digital currency assets are supported?
The trade form currently lists Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT on TRC-20, and USDT on ERC-20. Check the asset selector for the networks available to your order.
Security & Custody Standards
How are client cryptocurrency deposits secured?
Always verify the selected blockchain network and destination address before sending crypto. Never share account credentials or follow payment instructions from unverified messages.
Is Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) mandatory?
Use a unique password and keep your sign-in details private. Available account security controls are shown in your profile settings.
Pricing, Spreads & Fees
Are there hidden exchange fees or deposit surcharges?
The trade form and order review show the configured conversion and applicable network fee. Final order values are calculated using the active server rates when the order is submitted.
